# Roadmap

These are proposed milestones, not shipped capabilities or delivery commitments.

## 0.1 — Invention intake preview (this release)

- Self-contained skill and output conventions.
- Source-linked fictional example.
- Adversarial cases and evaluation rubric.
- Package integrity checks.

## 0.2 — Validate with practitioners

- Run recorded evaluations on named models.
- Ask patent counsel to review synthetic outputs.
- Measure unsupported assertions, missed contradictions, and editing effort.
- Improve source extraction and diagram review based on observed failures.

## Later — Filing review and portfolio management

- Configurable company scoring policies and deterministic budget scenarios.
- Portfolio import with family relationships, jurisdiction, claim status, and source dates.
- Technology buckets mapped to products, teams, and roadmap initiatives.
- Claim-informed overlap review and counsel-confirmed coverage maps.

## Separately scoped future skills

- Office-action issue mapping and response preparation.
- Claim-support review.
- Prosecution-history summaries.

External search, live interviewing, transcription, document exports, docketing, and professional-system integrations need their own evaluated implementations. They are not provided by this preview.
