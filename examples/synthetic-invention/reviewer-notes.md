# Review commentary

This example illustrates intended judgment, not measured performance.

## What a useful output preserves

- The battery figure is an untested expectation, not a measured result.
- The later transcript does not establish the final production design; it explicitly leaves selection open.
- Count/window precedence is a distinct technical gap from priority bypass.
- The receiver, clock synchronization, and lost-message fallback are not silently invented.
- The roadmap supports provisional strategic alignment but not proof of a portfolio gap.
- INT-017's title is a reason to retrieve claims, not proof of coverage or duplication.
- A named public demo is escalated for counsel review without calculating a filing deadline.
- A suggested alert-delay alternative is separated from inventor contributions.

## What still requires human review

Inventors must confirm the mechanism and contribution accounts. Counsel must assess legal questions, relevant prior art, timing, and portfolio claims. A technical reviewer should verify that the diagrams do not imply more than the sources support.

## How to compare a model response

Use `evaluations/rubric.md`. A different title, wording, or question order is acceptable. A response that resolves the conflict without evidence, invents a battery result, or declares the invention already covered is a substantive failure even if it looks polished.
